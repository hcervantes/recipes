# Chicken Shawarma 🌯

**From the video** by ThatDudeCanCook (Sonny): *I Could Eat This Chicken Shawarma Every Single Day*

Chicken thighs marinated overnight in a spiced yogurt marinade, seared hard, then wrapped in flatbread with a homemade yogurt-cucumber sauce.

## Ingredients

### Chicken & Marinade

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken thighs (boneless, skinless) | 2½ lbs |
| Plain yogurt | ½ cup |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 5 |
| Coriander powder | 1 tbsp |
| Cumin powder | 1 tbsp |
| Cardamom powder | 2 tsp |
| White vinegar | 1 tbsp |
| Red chili powder | 1 tbsp |
| Onion powder | 1 tbsp |
| Smoked paprika | 1 tbsp |
| Juice of 1 lemon | — |
| Olive oil | ¼ cup |
| Salt | 2 tsp |
| Black pepper | 2 tsp |

### Yogurt Sauce

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Plain yogurt | 1 cup |
| Grated cucumber, salted | ¾ cup |
| Garlic cloves, salted and pureed | 2 medium |
| Finely chopped cilantro | ½ cup |
| Coriander seed | ½ tsp |
| Cumin seed | ½ tsp |

### To Serve

- Flatbreads
- Red onion
- Tomato
- Crunchy lettuce
- Yogurt sauce + spicy sauce

## Instructions

1. **Marinate.** Whisk the yogurt, garlic, spices, vinegar, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper together. Coat the chicken thighs and marinate at least overnight in the fridge (a shorter marination works, but overnight gives maximum flavor).
2. **Make the yogurt sauce.** Grate and salt the cucumber, squeeze out excess moisture. Stir into the yogurt with pureed garlic, cilantro, coriander seed, and cumin seed. Chill.
3. **Sear.** Cook the marinated thighs over high heat (skillet or grill) until nicely charred and cooked through. Rest briefly, then slice or chop into strips.
4. **Warm the flatbreads** (fresh if possible).
5. **Assemble the wraps.** Layer chicken, lettuce, tomato, red onion, a generous drizzle of yogurt sauce, and spicy sauce. Roll it tight — "if it's not tight, it's not right!"

## Notes & Tips

- Chicken thighs stay juiciest; breasts can work but watch for dryness.
- Salt the cucumber and drain well, or the sauce gets watery.
- Pickled onions are a great addition (a popular suggestion from the comments).
- Vacuum-sealing the chicken speeds up marination if you're short on time.

# Juiciest Lamb Kabobs 🍢

**From the video** by The Mediterranean Dish (Suzy Karadsheh): *Juiciest Lamb Kabob Recipe*

- **Prep:** 15 minutes
- **Cook:** 10 minutes
- **Marinating:** 2 hours
- **Serves:** 6
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean / Middle Eastern

A bold onion-based marinade flavors *and* tenderizes the lamb, and high-heat grilling delivers juicy, succulent kabobs every time.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Boneless leg of lamb | 1½ lbs |
| Kosher salt and black pepper | to season |

### For the Marinade

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Yellow onion, medium | 1 |
| Garlic cloves | 5 |
| Allspice | 1 tsp |
| Nutmeg | ½ tsp |
| Cardamom | ½ tsp |
| Fresh parsley, packed | 1 cup |
| Extra virgin olive oil | ⅓ cup |
| Juice and zest of 1 lemon | — |

## Instructions

1. **Cut the lamb.** Trim excess fat and cut the meat into 1–1½-inch cubes. Place in a large bowl and season with kosher salt and black pepper.
2. **Make the marinade.** In a food processor, combine the onion, garlic, spices, parsley, olive oil, and lemon juice and zest. Blitz until finely chopped and thick.
3. **Marinate.** Pour over the lamb and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate up to 2 hours (or 30 minutes at room temperature if short on time).
4. **Skewer.** Brush grill grates (or an indoor griddle) with oil and heat. Shake off excess marinade and thread the lamb onto skewers, leaving a little space between pieces. (Soak bamboo skewers in water for at least 1 hour first; metal skewers need no soaking.)
5. **Grill.** Cook over high heat, turning a quarter turn every couple of minutes, until browned all over — 7 to 9 minutes depending on desired doneness. 7 minutes gives medium-rare on a hot grill.

## Notes & Tips

- **Best cuts:** boneless leg of lamb (used here), lamb tenderloin/loin fillet (most tender), or lamb shoulder (marinate 4–8 hours).
- **Don't skip marinating** — the pureed onion is the magic tenderizer.
- Trim excess fat to avoid flare-ups and burnt flavors.
- No grill? Use an indoor griddle, grill pan, or broiler — or spread the cubes on a baking sheet / air-fryer basket at 400°F until cooked through.
- Leftovers keep 2–3 days in the fridge.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve with tzatziki, hummus, white-bean hummus with dukkah, or toum, plus warm pita or flatbread. Add a fresh salad — Mediterranean tomato salad, Greek salad, panzanella, or watermelon salad.

# Easiest Same-Day Sourdough Bread in a Loaf Pan (No Dutch Oven, No Fuss!)

A beginner-friendly, no-knead, same-day sourdough bread baked in a loaf pan using the **2 loaf pan method** (one pan inverted as a lid) — no Dutch oven or special equipment needed. Works with active starter or unfed sourdough discard, so you don't have to time your feedings.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Sourdough starter (unfed/hungry **or** active) | 165 g | ¾ cup |
| Room-temperature water | 400 g | 1⅔ cups |
| All-purpose or bread flour | 650 g | 5⅓ cups |
| Salt | 15 g | 1 Tbsp |

## Instructions

1. **Mix.** Measure the starter (or discard) and water into a bowl and mix. Add the flour and salt, and stir until well combined — about 2–3 minutes. It will seem too dry at first; keep mixing by hand until all the flour is incorporated evenly.
2. **Bulk ferment.** Cover with wrap and let sit on the counter at room temperature for 3–12 hours, or until expanded and puffy.
3. **Shape in bowl.** Stretch the sides of the dough up and pull them to the middle until you have a round ball and the dough changes from stretchy to tight. Cover again and rest 1 hour on the counter.
4. **Pan and score.** Turn the dough out, lightly shape it into a loaf, and place in a well-greased loaf pan (a dough sling on the bottom helps). Score the dough so it doesn't burst while baking.
5. **Bake (cold oven start).** Invert a second loaf pan over the dough to act as a lid. Place in a **cold** oven, set to **450°F**, and bake for **45 minutes**.
6. **Finish.** Remove the top pan, reduce the oven to **425°F**, and bake another **15 minutes**, or until an internal thermometer reads **200–205°F**.
7. **Cool.** Remove the loaf from the pan immediately so the bottom doesn't burn. Let cool completely for the best texture and crumb before slicing.

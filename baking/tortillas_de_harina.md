# Flour Tortillas 🫓 (Tortillas de Harina)

**From your uploaded image.** Soft homemade flour tortillas — simple pantry ingredients, one bowl, and a hot skillet.

- **Prep:** 15 minutes + 30 min rest
- **Cook:** ~15 minutes
- **Yield:** 9–10 tortillas

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| All-purpose flour | 500 g (2½ cups) |
| Vegetable shortening or lard | ⅓ cup |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Baking powder | ½ tsp |
| Warm water (on the hotter side) | 1 cup |
| Warm milk | ½ cup |

## Instructions

1. **Mix dry ingredients.** In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and baking powder.
2. **Cut in the shortening.** Add the shortening or lard and work it in with your fingers or a pastry cutter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
3. **Add liquids and mix.** Pour in the warm water and warm milk, stirring until a soft, slightly sticky dough comes together. Knead a few minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. **Form balls.** Divide the dough into 9–10 equal portions and roll each into a smooth ball.
5. **Rest.** Cover the balls and let them rest for 30 minutes (this relaxes the gluten so they roll out easily).
6. **Roll and cook.** Roll each ball into a thin, round tortilla. Cook on a hot, dry skillet or comal — about 30–60 seconds per side — until golden spots form and the tortilla puffs. Stack them in a clean towel to stay soft.

## Tips

- Keep the water/milk genuinely warm — it helps the fat distribute and makes a softer dough.
- Don't skip the 30-minute rest, or the tortillas will shrink back when rolled.
- Pressing lightly on the tortilla with a spatula while it cooks encourages it to puff.
- Store in a sealed bag 2–3 days at room temp, or freeze with parchment between them.

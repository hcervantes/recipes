# Same-Day Sourdough Sandwich Bread

**Yield:** 1 loaf (approx. 20 × 10 cm pan)

**Total Time:** ~12 hours from feeding levain to finished bake

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **Levain (Feed early morning)**

* **60 g** Active sourdough starter / levain
* **60 g** Bread flour
* **60 g** Water

#### **Main Dough**

* **410 g** Bread flour (sifted)
* **170 g** Active levain (at peak)
* **100 g** Milk
* **120 g** Water *(add gradually; you may need slightly less)*
* **30 g** Honey or sugar *(use sugar if new to sourdough)*
* **5 g** Salt
* **50 g** Butter (softened)

#### **Egg Wash**

* **1** Whole egg
* **2 tbsp** Milk
* **1 pinch** Salt

---

### **Instructions**

1. **Feed the Levain:** Allow 4–6 hours to double.
In the early morning, mix 60 g starter, 60 g bread flour, and 60 g water [[00:24](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D24)]. Let it rise until it doubles in volume, which typically takes under 6 hours [[01:20](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D80)].


2. **Mix & Knead the Dough:**
In a stand mixer bowl (or by hand), combine 410 g sifted bread flour, 170 g active levain, 30 g honey or sugar, 100 g milk, and most of the 120 g water [[02:05](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D125)]. Add 5 g salt and mix [[02:41](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D161)]. Knead on low speed for about 15 minutes, adding the 50 g softened butter during kneading [[03:24](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D204)].


3. **Perform Windowpane Test & First Rest:**
Shape the dough into a smooth ball and perform a windowpane test to confirm proper gluten development [[04:41](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D281)]. Place in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and rest for 30 minutes [[05:10](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D310)].


4. **Stretch and Fold:**
Perform 3 to 4 sets of stretch-and-folds, resting 30 minutes between each set [[05:26](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D326)]. Stop when the dough feels strong and has visibly risen [[06:20](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D380)].


5. **Pre-shape & Bench Rest:**
Gently round the dough on the counter without tearing the surface [[06:39](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D399)]. Let it rest uncovered for 30 minutes [[07:02](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D422)].


6. **Shape & Pan:**
Lightly flour your work surface, flatten the dough gently, and roll it up tightly into a log [[07:20](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D440)]. Place seam-side down into a greased or non-stick 20 × 10 cm loaf pan [[08:46](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D526)].


7. **Final Rise:** 2 to 3 hours.
Cover and let rise for 2 to 3 hours until the dough reaches the rim of the pan or doubles in size [[08:51](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D531)]. Preheat your oven to 220°C (428°F) for 45 minutes [[09:15](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D555)].


8. **Bake & Cool:**
Whisk the egg wash ingredients together and brush over the dough [[09:20](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D560)]. Bake at 220°C (428°F) for 15 minutes, then reduce the heat to 170°C (340°F) and bake for an additional 25 to 30 minutes until deep golden brown [[09:45](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D585)]. Allow the loaf to cool for 2 to 24 hours before slicing [[10:07](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DNTACpv3S590%26t%3D607)].

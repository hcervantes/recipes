### **Simple Sourdough Recipe (Makes 2 Loaves)**
**Video Source:** [No More Flat Bread! My Simple Sourdough Method - YouTube](https://youtu.be/jTsi9q3hK5A?is=wjo6w9n3ybVoWIHv)

#### **Ingredients**
- **200g** active sourdough starter
- **700g** filtered room-temperature water
- **1000g** unbleached all-purpose flour
- **20g** sea salt

---

#### **Instructions**
1. **Mixing the Dough**
   - Combine the **sourdough starter**, **water**, and **flour** in a large mixing bowl.
   - Mix until no dry flour remains.
   - Let the dough rest for **30–60 minutes** (autolyse).
   - Add the **salt** and mix thoroughly.

2. **Bulk Fermentation**
   - Perform **stretch and folds** (sets 1 and 2) every **30 minutes** for the first **2 hours**.
   - Switch to **coil folds** (sets 3 and 4) for the next **2 hours**.
   - Let the dough rest and ferment for a total of **4–6 hours** at room temperature.

3. **Shaping**
   - Divide the dough into **2 equal portions**.
   - Shape each portion into a round loaf (boule) or oval (batard).
   - Place the shaped dough into **banneton baskets** (proofing baskets) lined with cloth.

4. **Proofing**
   - Cover the dough and let it proof in the refrigerator for **12–16 hours** (overnight).

5. **Scoring and Baking**
   - Preheat your oven to **450°F (230°C)** with a **Dutch oven** inside.
   - Turn the dough onto parchment paper, **score** the top with a razor or lame.
   - Bake in the preheated Dutch oven for **20 minutes** with the lid on.
   - Remove the lid and bake for another **20–25 minutes** until golden brown.

6. **Cooling**
   - Let the loaves cool on a wire rack for **at least 1 hour** before slicing.

# 🥞 Fluffy Pancakes

From the viral [Preppy Kitchen "FLUFFY Pancakes" video](https://youtu.be/9zCVCL4V8JQ) by John Kanell — tender centers, crisp edges, made with simple pantry staples.

**Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 15 min · **Total:** 25 min · **Yield:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1⅓ cups (160g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder (fresh — check the date!)
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk
- 1 large egg
- 4 tablespoons (56g) butter, melted (unsalted)
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. **Whisk dry:** In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. **Whisk wet:** In a medium bowl, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla until well combined.
3. **Combine:** Pour the wet mixture into the dry and fold just until combined. A few small lumps are okay — they actually help make fluffier pancakes!
4. **Heat:** Warm a large skillet or griddle over medium-high heat, then grease lightly with butter.
5. **Cook:** Working in batches, add ¼ cup of batter per pancake. Cook a few minutes until the bottom is golden and bubbles appear on top, then flip once and cook about 1 more minute until golden. Add butter between batches. Serve hot with maple syrup and a pat of butter.

## Tips for the Fluffiest Pancakes

- **Don't overmix** — a smooth batter over-develops the gluten and makes pancakes dense and chewy.
- **Weigh the flour** (or fluff, spoon, and level it) — too much flour makes dry pancakes.
- **Preheat, then lower the heat** — start with a hot pan, but cook over medium-low so they cook through without burning.
- **Flip only once** — wait until several bubbles pop and the bottom is golden. Don't press down with the spatula!
- **Don't let the batter sit** too long before cooking.
- **Keep warm** on a wire rack in a 200°F oven while you finish the batches.

## Storage & Reheating

- **Fridge:** Cool to room temperature first, then store airtight up to 4 days.
- **Freezer:** Stack cooled pancakes with parchment between each, wrap, and freeze up to 3 months.
- **Reheat:** Microwave 15–30 seconds, toaster, or air fryer at 300°F for 3–5 minutes.

## Variations

- Swap the milk for buttermilk (add a tablespoon or two extra) for a mild tang.
- Fold in blueberries, chocolate chips, chopped nuts, or sliced banana.
- Top with honey butter, whipped cream, fresh fruit, powdered sugar, lemon curd, or caramel sauce.

# 🍌 Best Banana Bread

Adapted from [The Salty Marshmallow's Best Banana Bread Recipe](https://thesaltymarshmallow.com/best-banana-bread-recipe/) by Nichole — a soft, moist, classic one-loaf banana bread and the perfect way to use up overripe bananas.

**Prep:** 15 min · **Bake:** 45–55 min · **Total:** ~1 hr 5 min · **Yield:** 1 loaf (9×5-inch pan)

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, melted
- 3 large ripe bananas (the darker and more overripe, the better)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp cinnamon

## Instructions

1. **Preheat** the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9×5-inch loaf pan with butter or non-stick spray.
2. **Melt** the butter in a large microwave-safe bowl, about 1 minute.
3. **Mash** the bananas into the melted butter with a fork until mostly smooth.
4. **Add** the eggs and vanilla; stir until no yellow streaks of egg remain.
5. **Whisk** the flour, sugar, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon in a second large bowl.
6. **Combine** wet and dry with a spatula, mixing just until combined — don't overmix.
7. **Bake** 45–55 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

## Tips & Notes

- **Riper = better.** Dark yellow, heavily browned bananas give the most moisture and banana flavor.
- **Storage:** Cool completely, wrap tightly in plastic wrap, then keep in a sealed bag or container — up to 3 days on the counter or 6 days in the fridge.
- **Freezing:** Wrap cooled bread in plastic wrap, then foil; freeze up to 2 months. Thaw covered on the counter ~24 hours.
- **Add-ins:** Fold in 1 cup of chopped nuts or chocolate chips before baking.
- **Muffins:** Use a standard muffin tin and bake 25–30 minutes instead.
- **Substitutions:** Stick with vanilla extract — almond extract overpowers the banana. Banana extract works but tastes more artificial.

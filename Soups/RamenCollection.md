## 1. Tonkotsu Ramen

### Ingredients

#### Broth
* 2 lbs pork trotters
* 2 lbs pork bones
* 10 cups water (filtered preferred)

#### Seasoning Tare (Optional)
* ¼ cup soy sauce
* 2 tbsp mirin
* 2 tbsp miso paste

#### Serving
* Fresh ramen noodles
* Soft-boiled ramen eggs (ajitsuke tamago)
* Sliced green onions / scallions
* Nori sheets

---

### Instructions

1. **Clean the Bones:** Rinse the pork trotters and bones under cold running water. Place them in a large stockpot and submerge completely in fresh cold water.
2. **Initial Boil:** Bring the pot to a rapid boil over high heat. Let boil vigorously for 10 minutes to release impurities and scum.
3. **Drain and Rinse:** Drain the pot completely into a colander, discarding all blanching water. Rinse each bone and trotter thoroughly under cold running water to remove any residual clotted marrow and scum. Wash out the pot.
4. **Long Simmer:** Return cleaned bones to the pot and cover with 10 cups of fresh filtered water. Bring back to a boil, then reduce to a steady, rolling simmer for at least 6 hours. Add boiling water as needed to keep bones submerged.
5. **Emulsify:** The broth will gradually shift from translucent to an opaque, milky white as gelatin, collagen, and fats fully emulsify.
6. **Strain and Season:** Strain the broth through a fine-mesh sieve. Stir in seasoning tare to taste.
7. **Assemble:** Pour steaming broth over cooked, well-drained ramen noodles. Top with soft-boiled egg halves, sliced scallions, and nori sheets.

## 2. Classic Shoyu Ramen

### Ingredients

#### Dashi & Soup Stock
* 1000 ml water
* 10 g dried sardines (*niboshi*), heads and guts removed
* 5 g dried kelp (*kombu*)
* 4 chicken wings (scored along the bone on the skinless side)
* 2 cloves garlic, crushed
* 20 g fresh ginger, sliced
* 1 Japanese leek (*naganegi*), green tops only
* 5 g bonito flakes (*katsuobushi*)

#### Shoyu Tare (Seasoning Base)
* 1 tbsp sake
* 1 tbsp mirin
* ½ tsp sugar
* 1 pinch salt
* 3 tbsp water
* 3 tbsp Japanese dark soy sauce (*koikuchi shoyu*)

#### Aroma Oil (Chicken Fat)
* 50 g chicken skin
* ½ tbsp lard or neutral cooking oil

#### Assembly & Toppings
* 2 portions fresh ramen noodles
* Sliced pork chashu
* Soft-boiled ramen eggs
* Seasoned bamboo shoots (*menma*)
* Sliced narutomaki (fish cake)
* Nori sheets
* Chopped green onions

---

### Instructions

1. **Cold-Infuse Dashi:** Add water, dried sardines, and kombu to a pot. Heat gently to 60 °C (140 °F) and maintain for 30 minutes.
2. **Prepare Tare:** In a small saucepan, combine sake, mirin, sugar, and salt. Simmer over low heat until sugar dissolves and alcohol cooks off. Stir in water and soy sauce; simmer gently for 5–10 minutes. Set aside.
3. **Simmer Chicken Broth:** Remove kombu and sardines from the dashi pot. Add chicken wings, crushed garlic, sliced ginger, and leek greens. Heat until faint bubbles form (80 °C–85 °C / 176 °F–185 °F). Skim surface foam for the first 10 minutes, then steep on low for 40 minutes without boiling.
4. **Render Chicken Oil:** In a skillet over low heat, melt lard and spread chicken skin flat. Cover with aluminum foil and press down with a heavy pot lid. Cook for ~10 minutes per side until golden and fat has rendered. Strain the clear chicken fat into a small bowl.
5. **Finish Stock:** Turn off stockpot heat. Add bonito flakes and steep undisturbed for 5 minutes. Strain the broth through a paper-towel-lined fine sieve.
6. **Assemble Bowls:** Pre-warm serving bowls with hot water, then discard water. Add 2 tablespoons of tare and 1 tablespoon of rendered chicken oil to each bowl. Pour in ~300 ml of hot broth and whisk lightly. Drop in freshly boiled, well-drained noodles. Top with chashu, eggs, menma, narutomaki, scallions, and nori.

## 3. Fiery Chicken Ramen with Garlic Cream Sauce

### Ingredients

* 2 portions ramen noodles
* 2 boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
* 2 tbsp chili paste or sambal oelek (adjust to heat preference)
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 cup heavy cream
* 2 cups fresh baby spinach
* 2 green onions, thinly sliced
* Olive oil or neutral cooking oil
* Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
* *Optional garnish:* Toasted sesame seeds, extra chili flakes

---

### Instructions

1. **Cook Noodles:** Boil ramen noodles according to package directions until al dente. Drain, rinse briefly under cool water to halt cooking, and set aside.
2. **Sear Chicken:** Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet or wok over medium heat. Season chicken with salt and pepper, then sauté until golden brown and cooked through (5–7 minutes).
3. **Aromatics:** Add minced garlic and chili paste directly to the pan with the chicken. Sauté for 60 seconds until fragrant.
4. **Build Sauce:** Pour in heavy cream, stirring continuously to deglaze pan fond. Bring to a gentle simmer on low heat until sauce begins to thicken slightly.
5. **Wilt Greens:** Fold in baby spinach and half of the green onions; cook until spinach has just wilted (about 1 minute).
6. **Toss and Serve:** Add cooked noodles directly to the pan. Toss thoroughly to coat every strand in sauce. Garnish with remaining green onions and toasted sesame seeds.

## 4. Marry Me Chicken Ramen

### Ingredients

#### Chicken & Base
* 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
* 50 g all-purpose flour (for dredging)
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
* 8 oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, drained and thinly sliced
* ½ tbsp smoked paprika
* ½ tbsp dried oregano
* ½ tbsp red pepper chili flakes
* 600 ml chicken stock (split: 300 ml + 300 ml)
* 300 ml single cream (or half-and-half)
* 2 tbsp freshly grated Parmesan cheese (plus extra for serving)
* ½ tsp granulated sugar
* ½ tsp flaky sea salt
* ½ tsp freshly cracked black pepper

#### Noodles & Garnish
* 200 g ramen noodles
* Fresh coriander (cilantro)
* Fresh bean sprouts
* Steamed edamame beans
* Sliced spring onions
* Chili oil

---

### Instructions

1. **Dredge & Sear:** Lightly coat chicken breasts in flour, shaking off excess. Heat olive oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Sear chicken for 4–5 minutes per side until golden brown; transfer to a plate.
2. **Sauté Aromatics:** In the same skillet, add minced garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, smoked paprika, oregano, and chili flakes. Cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
3. **Create Cream Base:** Pour in 300 ml chicken stock, followed by the cream and grated Parmesan. Whisk until smooth.
4. **Simmer Chicken:** Return chicken breasts to the sauce, cover with a lid, and simmer on low for 5–7 minutes until internal temperature registers 165 °F (74 °C). Transfer chicken to a cutting board and slice into strips.
5. **Season Sauce:** Stir sugar, salt, and black pepper into the cream sauce.
6. **Cook Noodles:** Pour the remaining 300 ml of chicken stock into the skillet and bring to a simmer. Add ramen noodles and cook directly in the sauce until tender.
7. **Assemble:** Ladle noodles and creamy broth into bowls. Arrange sliced chicken, bean sprouts, edamame, spring onions, fresh coriander, and a drizzle of chili oil on top.

## 5. Spicy Braised Beef Ramen

### Ingredients

#### Braised Beef & Broth
* 500 g beef chuck roast or brisket, cut into 1.5-inch cubes
* 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
* 4 cloves garlic, smashed
* 1-inch fresh ginger knob, sliced
* 1 tsp whole Sichuan peppercorns
* 1 whole cinnamon stick
* 2 whole star anise pods
* 2 tbsp light soy sauce
* 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
* 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
* 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
* 1 tsp granulated sugar
* 2–3 tbsp chili oil (with sediment)
* 4 cups beef broth or water
* Salt to taste

#### Noodles & Assembly
* 200–250 g fresh ramen or wheat noodles
* 2–3 heads baby bok choy, halved lengthwise
* 2 soft-boiled ramen eggs, halved
* 2 green onions, finely sliced
* Extra chili oil for drizzling

---

### Instructions

1. **Brown Beef:** Heat cooking oil in a Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat. Sear beef cubes until deeply browned on all sides (6–8 minutes).
2. **Toast Spices:** Push beef to one side of the pot. Add garlic, ginger, Sichuan peppercorns, cinnamon stick, and star anise; sauté in residual oil for 1 minute.
3. **Deglaze & Sauce:** Pour in Shaoxing wine, scraping up browned bits from the bottom. Stir in light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, hoisin sauce, sugar, and chili oil.
4. **Slow Braise:** Pour in beef broth. Bring to a boil, then drop heat to low, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and simmer gently for 1½ to 2 hours until beef is fork-tender. Season with salt as needed.
5. **Blanch Greens & Cook Noodles:** Boil noodles in a separate pot according to package directions, drain, and divide between bowls. Blanch bok choy in boiling water for 90 seconds until vibrant green and tender-crisp.
6. **Serve:** Ladle rich beef broth over noodles. Top each bowl with tender braised beef chunks, blanched bok choy, half a soft-boiled egg, scallions, and chili oil.

## 6. Korean Fire Chicken (Buldak) Cheese Ramen

### Ingredients

#### Fire Chicken (Buldak)
* 300 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized strips
* 2 tbsp gochujang (Korean red pepper paste)
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tbsp honey
* 1 tbsp white granulated sugar
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
* 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
* 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil

#### Ramen Base
* 2 packs instant or fresh ramen noodles
* 700 ml water or light chicken broth
* 1 tsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp garlic powder

#### Cheese Melt & Garnish
* 1 cup shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese
* 2 tbsp heavy cream (optional, for extra smooth melt)
* 2 soft-boiled eggs
* Sliced scallions
* Toasted sesame seeds
* Chili oil

---

### Instructions

1. **Marinate Chicken:** In a mixing bowl, combine chicken strips, gochujang, soy sauce, honey, sugar, garlic, gochugaru, and sesame oil. Mix thoroughly and marinate for at least 15 minutes.
2. **Pan-Sear Buldak:** Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add marinated chicken and sauté until caramelized, slightly charred on the edges, and cooked through (6–8 minutes).
3. **Par-Cook Noodles:** In a separate pot, boil water or broth with 1 tsp soy sauce and 1 tsp garlic powder. Cook ramen noodles for 2 minutes (just shy of fully cooked). Drain, reserving about ¼ cup of starchy cooking liquid.
4. **Combine:** Transfer noodles and reserved cooking water into the skillet with the fire chicken. Toss over medium heat for 1 minute until sauce glazes the noodles.
5. **Melt Cheese:** Reduce heat to low. Scatter shredded mozzarella (and splash of heavy cream, if using) evenly over the top. Cover with a lid for 90–120 seconds until cheese is completely melted and bubbly.
6. **Serve:** Serve hot directly from the skillet or transfer to bowls. Top with halved soft-boiled eggs, green onions, toasted sesame seeds, and chili oil.

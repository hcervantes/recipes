## 1. Tonkotsu Ramen

### Ingredients

#### For the Broth
- 2 lbs Pork trotters
- 2 lbs Pork bones
- 10 cups Water

#### For Seasoning (Optional)
- 1/4 cup Soy sauce
- 2 tbsp Mirin
- 2 tbsp Miso

### Instructions
1. **Prepare the Pork:** Rinse pork trotters and bones under cold water. Place in a large pot and cover with enough water to fully submerge.
2. **Bring to a Boil:** Heat on high until vigorously boiling, then reduce heat to a gentle simmer and cook for about 10 minutes.
3. **Discard Water:** Drain the pot completely. Rinse bones and trotters under cold running water to remove impurities and debris.
4. **Simmer Broth:** Return cleaned bones and trotters to the pot with fresh filtered water. Bring to a boil again, then reduce heat and simmer for approximately 6 hours.
5. **Check Consistency:** The broth will transform from clear to a rich, milky white as gelatin and fat emulsify.
6. **Serve:** Strain broth and serve hot over cooked ramen noodles with scallions, soft-boiled eggs, and sheets of nori.

## 2. Shoyu Ramen

### Ingredients

#### Broth & Aromatics
- 2 portions Ramen noodles (medium-thin to medium-thick)
- 1000 ml Water
- 10 g Dried sardines (*niboshi*), heads and gut sacs removed
- 5 g Dried kelp (*kombu* or Korean *dashima*)
- 4 Chicken wings, scored along the bone on the skinless side
- 2 Garlic cloves, lightly smashed
- 20 g Ginger root, sliced
- 1 Japanese leek (*naganegi*), green tops only (or regular leek tops)
- 5 g Bonito flakes (*katsuobushi*)
- ½ tbsp Lard or neutral oil
- 50 g Chicken skin (or store-bought chicken oil, ½–1 tbsp per bowl)

#### Tare (Seasoning Base)
- 1 tbsp Sake (or dry sherry / white wine)
- 1 tbsp Mirin
- ½ tsp Sugar
- 1 pinch Salt
- 3 tbsp Water
- 3 tbsp Japanese soy sauce (*koikuchi shoyu*)

#### Suggested Toppings
- Sliced pork chashu
- Ramen egg (halved) or soft-boiled egg
- Seasoned bamboo shoots (*menma*)
- Sliced *narutomaki* fish cake
- Roasted seaweed (*nori*)
- Finely chopped green onions

### Instructions
1. **Dashi Base:** In a pot, combine 1000 ml water, 10 g dried sardines, and 5 g kelp. Hold at 60 °C (140 °F) for 30 minutes.
2. **Make the Tare:** In a small saucepan, stir together sake, mirin, sugar, and salt. Simmer until sugar and salt dissolve and alcohol burns off. Add 3 tbsp water and 3 tbsp soy sauce; simmer on low for 5–10 minutes until slightly thickened. Store in a sealable container.
3. **Broth (Part 1):** Remove kombu and sardines from dashi. Add chicken wings, garlic, ginger, and leek tops. Heat on medium until small bubbles appear (do not boil). Skim scum for 10 minutes, then hold at 80–85 °C (176–185 °F) for 40 minutes.
4. **Render Chicken Fat:** Melt ½ tbsp lard in a frying pan on low. Lay chicken skin flat, cover with foil and a heavy weight, and fry ~10 minutes per side until crisp and fat has rendered. Pour fat into a heatproof bowl.
5. **Broth (Part 2):** Turn off heat under broth, add bonito flakes, and steep for 5 minutes. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with paper towel.
6. **Assembly:** Cook noodles ~20 seconds less than package directions. Warm bowls with hot water, empty, and divide tare evenly. Pour ~300 ml hot broth per bowl, stir, drop in noodles, drizzle rendered chicken fat, and add toppings.

## 3. Fiery Chicken Ramen with Garlic Sauce

### Ingredients
- Ramen noodles (2 portions)
- 2 Boneless chicken thighs, chopped into small pieces
- 2 tbsp Chili paste (adjust to taste)
- 4 Garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1 cup Heavy cream
- 2 cups Fresh spinach leaves
- 2 Green onions, thinly sliced
- Olive oil
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### Instructions
1. **Cook Noodles:** Boil ramen noodles according to package directions. Drain, rinse briefly with cool water to halt cooking, and set aside.
2. **Brown Chicken:** Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add chicken pieces, season with salt and pepper, and cook 5–7 minutes until cooked through and browned.
3. **Build Flavor:** Add minced garlic and chili paste. Sauté for ~1 minute until fragrant (do not burn garlic).
4. **Make Sauce Creamy:** Slowly pour in heavy cream while stirring. Simmer gently until sauce thickens and coats chicken.
5. **Wilt Greens:** Add spinach and half the green onions; stir until spinach wilts (1–2 minutes).
6. **Combine:** Add cooked noodles directly to the pan and toss until evenly coated. Heat through for 1–2 minutes.
7. **Serve:** Divide into bowls and garnish with remaining green onions and optional chili flakes or sesame seeds.

## 4. Marry Me Chicken Ramen

### Ingredients
- 2 tbsp Olive oil
- 2 Chicken breasts
- 50 g All-purpose flour
- 3 Garlic cloves, minced
- 8 Sun-dried tomatoes, sliced
- ½ tbsp Smoked paprika
- ½ tbsp Oregano
- ½ tbsp Chili flakes
- 600 ml Chicken stock (divided: 300 ml + 300 ml)
- 300 ml Single cream
- 2 tbsp Grated parmesan (plus extra for serving)
- ½ tsp Ground black pepper
- ½ tsp Flaky salt
- ½ tsp Sugar
- 200 g Ramen noodles

#### Optional Toppings
- Fresh coriander
- Bean sprouts
- Edamame beans
- Sliced spring onions
- Chili oil

### Instructions
1. Dredge chicken breasts lightly in flour.
2. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Sear chicken ~4 minutes per side until golden; remove and rest.
3. In the same pan, add garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, paprika, chili flakes, and oregano; fry for 2 minutes.
4. Pour in 300 ml chicken stock and cream, then stir in parmesan.
5. Return chicken to pan, cover, and simmer for 5 minutes.
6. Remove chicken and slice. Season sauce with salt, pepper, and sugar.
7. Pour in the remaining 300 ml chicken stock and add ramen noodles; cook per package instructions.
8. Plate noodles and broth into bowls, top with sliced chicken, extra parmesan, and desired toppings.

## 5. Spicy Beef Ramen

### Ingredients

#### For the Braised Beef
- 500 g Beef chuck or brisket, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tbsp Cooking oil
- 4 Garlic cloves, minced
- 1-inch Ginger piece, sliced
- 2 tbsp Soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 1 tsp Sugar
- 2–3 tbsp Chili oil
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
- 1 Cinnamon stick
- 2 Star anise
- 4 cups Beef broth or water
- Salt, to taste

#### For Serving
- 200–250 g Wheat or egg ramen noodles
- 2–3 stalks Bok choy (or pechay)
- 2 Soft-boiled eggs, halved
- 2–3 Green onions, chopped
- Chili oil

### Instructions
1. **Brown Beef:** Heat cooking oil in a pot over medium-high heat. Sear beef pieces until browned on all sides.
2. **Aromatics:** Add garlic, ginger, Sichuan peppercorns, cinnamon stick, and star anise; stir-fry for 1 minute.
3. **Seasoning:** Pour in soy sauce, dark soy sauce, hoisin, Shaoxing wine, sugar, and chili oil.
4. **Braise:** Add beef broth. Bring to a boil, then cover, turn heat to low, and simmer for 1.5 to 2 hours until tender.
5. **Noodles & Greens:** Boil noodles separately until al dente; drain. Blanch bok choy in hot water for 1–2 minutes.
6. **Serve:** Place noodles in deep bowls, ladle braised beef and broth over top, and arrange bok choy, soft-boiled egg halves, green onions, and chili oil.

## 6. Korean Fire Chicken Cheese Ramen

### Ingredients

#### Fire Chicken
- 300 g Boneless chicken thighs, sliced
- 2 tbsp Gochujang (Korean red pepper paste)
- 1 tbsp Soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Honey
- 1 tbsp Sugar
- 3 Garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp Gochugaru / chili flakes
- 1 tsp Toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp Vegetable oil

#### Noodles & Sauce
- 2 packs Instant ramen noodles (seasoning packet optional/discarded)
- 700 ml Water or chicken broth
- 1 tsp Soy sauce
- 1 tsp Garlic powder

#### Cheese Layer & Garnish
- 1 cup Shredded mozzarella cheese
- 2 tbsp Heavy cream (optional)
- Soft-boiled eggs, sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and chili oil

### Instructions
1. **Marinate:** In a bowl, combine chicken thighs with gochujang, soy sauce, honey, sugar, garlic, chili flakes, and sesame oil. Marinate for at least 15 minutes.
2. **Cook Chicken:** Heat vegetable oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and sauté until caramelized, crispy around the edges, and coated in thick sauce.
3. **Cook Noodles:** In a pot, bring broth/water to a boil with 1 tsp soy sauce and garlic powder. Add noodles and cook until chewy; drain, keeping a few tablespoons of liquid.
4. **Combine:** Transfer noodles and reserved cooking liquid directly into the skillet with the fire chicken; toss to coat evenly.
5. **Melt Cheese:** Lower heat to low, scatter mozzarella over noodles and chicken, cover with a lid, and heat 1–2 minutes until completely melted.
6. **Serve:** Garnish with eggs, scallions, sesame seeds, and chili oil. Serve immediately while cheese is melted and stretchy.

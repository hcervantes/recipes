# Green Chicken Chili 🌶️

**From the video** by Chili Pepper Madness (Mike Hultquist): *The BEST Green Chicken Chili (Fan Favorite!)*

- **Prep:** 15 minutes
- **Cook:** 40 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- Per serving: 464 kcal · 56 g protein · 31 g carbs · 8 g fiber

Chunks of tender chicken simmered in a tangy, from-scratch salsa verde with white beans and warm spices — done in well under an hour, with a slow cooker option.

## Ingredients

### For the Verde Sauce

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tomatillos, husked and roughly chopped | 2 lbs |
| Roasted green peppers, chopped (Hatch, poblanos, or a mix; canned OK) | 1 lb |
| Jalapeño peppers, chopped (optional — serranos for hotter) | 2–3 |

### For the Chili

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Vegetable oil | 2 tbsp |
| Boneless chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces (thighs, or thigh + breast mix) | 3 lbs |
| Onion, large, chopped | 1 |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 4–5 |
| Green chili powder (Hatch or any green blend; red chili powder works but dulls the color) | 1 tbsp |
| Mexican oregano | 1 tbsp |
| Ground cumin | 1 tsp |
| Salt and pepper | to taste |
| Chicken stock | ½–1 cup |
| Canned white beans, drained (cannellini; 2 cans for a heartier chili) | 14 oz |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | ½ cup |
| Juice of 1 large lime | — |

## Instructions (Stovetop)

1. **Make the verde sauce.** Blend the tomatillos, roasted green peppers, and jalapeños in a blender or food processor until smooth. Set aside. (Makes ~4 cups.)
2. **Sear the chicken.** Heat the oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and sear in batches, 2–3 minutes per side, until browned. Set aside.
3. **Sauté.** Cook the onion in the pot for 5 minutes to soften. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in the chili powder, Mexican oregano, cumin, and salt and pepper; cook 1 minute.
4. **Simmer.** Add the verde sauce, reserved chicken with its juices, chicken stock, and white beans. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer at least 30 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (longer simmering deepens the flavor).
5. **Finish.** Off the heat, stir in the fresh cilantro and lime juice. Garnish and serve.

## Slow Cooker Version

1. Blend the verde sauce and pour it into the slow cooker.
2. (Optional) Sear the chicken with the onion and garlic in a skillet, 3–4 minutes, and add to the slow cooker — or just add everything raw to save time.
3. Stir in remaining ingredients. Cover and cook on **low 5–6 hours** or **high 3–4 hours** until the chicken shreds easily.
4. Shred the chicken right in the pot, stir, and serve.

## Tips & Variations

- **Adjust heat:** more/fewer jalapeños or serranos; keep it mild for kids.
- **Soupier chili:** replace 1–2 cups of the verde sauce with chicken stock (extra verde freezes well).
- **Creamy version:** swirl in sour cream or cream cheese before serving.
- **Shortcuts:** use a rotisserie chicken (add at the end to warm through) or store-bought diced green chilies.
- **Popular add-ins:** 1 cup fresh or frozen corn, black beans, or mash some beans for a creamier body.
- **Roasting the peppers and tomatillos first** adds sweetness and depth.
- **Leftovers:** 5 days in the fridge; freezes 2–3 months.

## Serving Suggestions

Top with tortilla chips or strips, sour cream or crema, avocado, cilantro, green onions, sliced jalapeños, cotija/queso fresco/Monterey Jack, and a squeeze of lime. Serve with warm tortillas, Mexican cornbread, or Mexican rice. Pairs well with a crisp Mexican lager or a margarita.

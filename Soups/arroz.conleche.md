# Creamy Arroz con Leche 🍚🥛

**From the video (Short)** by Maria Ximena: *How to Make Creamy Arroz con Leche Just Like Mom Did 💕*

A classic Latin American rice pudding — rice simmered with cinnamon, then enriched with three milks until thick and creamy.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Long-grain white rice | 2 cups |
| Water | 6 cups |
| Cinnamon sticks | 2 |
| Sweetened condensed milk | 1 can (14 oz) |
| Evaporated milk | 1 can (12 oz) |
| Whole milk | 2 cups |
| Sugar | ⅓ cup (adjust to taste) |

Optional: raisins, ground cinnamon for topping, splash of vanilla.

## Instructions

1. **Simmer the rice.** In a large pot, combine the rice, water, and cinnamon sticks. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until the rice is tender and most of the water is absorbed (about 15–20 minutes). Remove the cinnamon sticks.
2. **Add the milks.** Stir in the evaporated milk, whole milk, and sweetened condensed milk.
3. **Thicken.** Continue simmering over medium-low heat, stirring frequently (it scorches easily on the bottom), for about 10–15 minutes until it thickens to a creamy pudding consistency. Remember it thickens more as it cools.
4. **Sweeten and serve.** Add the sugar, stirring until dissolved — taste and adjust (many find the condensed milk sweet enough on its own). Serve warm or chilled, dusted with ground cinnamon.

## Tips

- **Stir often** once the milks go in — the sugars scorch quickly.
- Slightly under-thicken on the stove; it firms up in the fridge.
- Raisins are a traditional stir-in (soak them first if you like them plump).
- Coconut milk version: swap the milks for coconut milk + sweetened condensed coconut milk (a popular variation).
- Leftovers keep 3–4 days refrigerated; loosen with a splash of milk when reheating.
